06 — Innovation spotlight

Carrying the grid by boat to Mousumi Island.

A 10 kW off-grid power plant, built over 35 days, brought electricity for the first time to 17 families on Bakkhali island in the Sundarbans — led by three NRM master trainers and three solar technicians under the guidance of Mr Bishnu Sarkar, DIPI Solar Solution.

STEP 01

Ferrying the panels

Eighteen 590W panels and system hardware crossed by country boat — the only way in or out of the island.

STEP 02

Building the power house

A single power house was wired with a 5,500W inverter and two 48V lithium battery sets, feeding two distribution boxes.

STEP 03

Switching on

Each of the 17 homes was wired for a 230V AC line — enough to run a fan and two LED bulbs through the darkest night.

Also this year

Greening the riverbank at Bhasraghat.

Around the Bhasraghat sand-mining site on the Subarnarekha River, 200 cashew, kadam and acacia saplings went into the ground — a joint effort of Gram Panchayat officials, the Forest Department and sand-mine owners to stabilise riverbanks and cut dust and soil erosion. Across the project, plantation drives covered 1 acre, engaged 152 community members, and six new nurseries raised 300 saplings sourced from the Forest Department.
